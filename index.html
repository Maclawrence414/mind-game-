How to Play

1
Mental Arithmetic Under Pressure: Evaluate the math equation shown on the screen and tap or press the corresponding correct answer button.
2
Beat the Neon Timer: The top progress bar drains quickly. Faster answers earn bigger speed reaction bonuses!
3
Streak Multipliers:
  • 3+ Streak: 1.5x Multiplier
  • 6+ Streak: 2.0x Multiplier
  • 10+ Streak: 3.0x Multiplier
  • 15+ Streak: 4.0x NEON OVERDRIVE
4
Keyboard Shortcuts: Use 1 2 3 4 (or NumPad / A B C D) to answer instantly. Use Space to start / replay, and Esc to pause.
5
Survival Rules: In Classic Mode, you have 3 lives. Every wrong answer or timeout costs 1 life. In 60s Blitz, race against the master clock!
